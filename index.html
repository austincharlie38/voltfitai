FAQ

Questions, answered.

What do the scores measure?

VoltAI looks at health and fitness signals: skin clarity and texture, under-eye puffiness and grooming for your face, and body composition, muscle development, posture and balance for your body. Every score comes with an explanation and a concrete next step.

What photos do I need?

One face photo and one body photo. Wear clothes you'd work out in; VoltAI only accepts clothed photos. Good, even lighting gives the most accurate results.

Do I need an account?

No. There's nothing to sign up for. Everything you create stays on your phone.

Are my photos stored anywhere?

No. Photos are sent securely to be analyzed and are not stored on our servers. See the privacy policy for the details.

What's included for free?

Your first scan, with full face and body scores and the breakdown. More scans, personal plans and Fridge AI are part of VoltAI Pro.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose VoltAI. You can cancel anytime; Pro stays active until the end of the period you've paid for.

Is this medical advice?

No. VoltAI provides general wellness guidance. Talk to a doctor before starting a new diet or exercise program.
