VoltAI Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

The short version

VoltAI has no accounts. Your profile, results, plans, saved recipes, and meal scans are stored only on your iPhone.

VoltAI's AI analysis does not run on your phone. When you start a scan, build a plan, use Fridge AI, or scan a meal, and only after you've given permission in the app, the photos and details that feature needs are sent over an encrypted connection to our server and on to our AI provider, Anthropic (the company behind the Claude AI model). Your result comes back to your phone.

VoltAI does not store your photos or results on any server. Anthropic does not use them to train AI and deletes them within 30 days, unless they're flagged for a safety review.

We don't sell your data, show ads, use analytics, or track you across apps and websites.

Information stored on your iPhone

VoltAI keeps the following only on your iPhone, in the app's private storage:

  • Your onboarding answers: name (optional), birthday, goals, sex, activity level, diet, allergies, plan length, and anything you write about your goals.
  • Your scan results, including small copies of your face and body photos so you can compare progress.
  • Your improvement plan, checklist progress, reminder settings, and recipes you save.

We can't see or access this information. It's included in your iPhone's own backups if you use iCloud or computer backups, under Apple's privacy terms. Deleting a scan removes it; deleting the app removes everything.

AI analysis and your permission

VoltAI's scans, plans, Fridge AI, and meal scans are powered by artificial intelligence that runs on a server, not on your device. Before anything is sent, VoltAI asks for your permission on a screen that explains what's sent, where it goes, and how long it's kept. If you don't agree, nothing is sent, and the AI features stay off. You can withdraw permission anytime in Profile → Privacy → AI processing.

Data is sent only when you start one of these features yourself:

  • Face and body scan: the face and body photos you take or choose, plus your age, sex, activity level, goals, plan length, and goal description.
  • Improvement plan: the profile details above, your diet and allergies, your workout survey answers (muscles you want to build, equipment, workout days per week, and lifting experience), and a summary of your latest scan (scores and suggestions; no photos).
  • Fridge AI: the food photos you take or choose (fridge, freezer, pantry, or single items), any food items you type, and your age, goals, diet, allergies, and latest scan priorities.
  • Meal scan: the photo of your meal, any note you add about it (for example "cooked in butter"), and your age, goals, and diet.

Photos are resized and compressed on your phone before sending. They travel over an encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connection to VoltAI's server, which forwards them to Anthropic, receives the result, and returns it to your phone. VoltAI's server holds your photos and details in memory only while it processes your request (usually under a minute) and then discards them. It doesn't save them to a database, file storage, or logs. Your result is saved only on your phone.

We use this information only to create the result you asked for. We don't use it for advertising, profiling, or training AI models.

Face and body photos

  • VoltAI's AI looks at visible fitness and skin-health signals (such as posture, muscle development, skin clarity, and under-eye puffiness) to produce estimated scores and tips. It never rates attractiveness.
  • VoltAI does not use facial recognition, does not create face geometry, faceprints, or other biometric identifiers, and does not use photos to identify you.
  • VoltAI never accesses Face ID or Touch ID data.
  • VoltAI only analyzes photos of people in clothing (for example, gym wear). The AI asks you to retake photos that show nudity or underwear only.
  • Your photos are never shared with anyone except Anthropic, for the sole purpose of producing your result.

Who processes your data

We use two service providers to run VoltAI's AI features. They act on our behalf, under contracts that limit how they can use your data:

  • Supabase hosts VoltAI's server (in the United States), which receives AI requests and forwards them, and stores waitlist emails from our website.
  • Anthropic provides the Claude AI model that analyzes photos and writes results. Under its commercial terms, Anthropic doesn't train its models on this data and deletes it within 30 days, except where it's flagged for trust and safety review or must be kept by law. See Anthropic's privacy policy.

Apple processes subscription payments (see below). These providers may automatically log technical details such as IP addresses to operate and secure their services.

We don't sell, rent, or share your personal information for advertising or cross-context behavioral advertising. We'd only disclose information if required by law, and since we don't store your photos or results, we generally have nothing to disclose.

How long data is kept

  • On your iPhone: until you delete it or the app.
  • VoltAI's server: photos and details aren't stored; they're discarded as soon as your result is returned. Technical logs (time of request, whether it succeeded, how much processing it used) contain no photos or answers and are kept only briefly for reliability and security.
  • Usage records: kept 30 days; the record of your one free scan is kept up to 12 months (see Fair use and abuse prevention).
  • Anthropic: deleted within 30 days, unless flagged for a safety review or legally required to be kept longer.
  • Waitlist emails: until we stop sending launch news or you ask us to remove yours.

Fair use and abuse prevention

AI analysis costs us money each time it runs, so VoltAI's server limits how often each feature can be used and makes sure the free scan is used only once. To do that, each AI request includes:

  • A random app install ID. It's created by VoltAI on your iPhone, isn't connected to your name, Apple ID, or device hardware, and is never used for advertising or tracking. It's stored in your iPhone's Keychain, so it can stay the same if you reinstall the app.
  • Your IP address, which we immediately turn into a scrambled (hashed) value and store only in that form, to stop one network from making unlimited requests.
  • If you subscribe: Apple's signed proof of your subscription (the product, dates, and Apple's transaction number; no name, email, or payment details). Our server checks it with Apple's public certificates and doesn't store it.

We keep a record of which feature was used, when, whether you're subscribed, and what the request cost us to process, linked only to the install ID and hashed IP, for 30 days. The record that a free scan was used is kept up to 12 months. We use these records only to enforce limits and prevent abuse.

What we don't collect

  • No account, email, password, or login in the app.
  • No location, contacts, microphone, or health data from Apple Health.
  • No advertising identifiers (IDFA), no analytics or tracking tools, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites. The only identifier is the random install ID described above.
  • No payment details (Apple handles payments).

Camera, photos, and notifications

  • Camera: used only when you choose to take a face, body, or food photo.
  • Photos: VoltAI uses Apple's photo picker, so the app only receives the photos you select. It can't see the rest of your library.
  • Notifications: plan, streak, and scan reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. No notification data is sent to us.

You can change these permissions anytime in your iPhone's Settings → VoltAI.

Subscriptions

VoltAI Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes payment using your Apple ID; we never receive your name, email, or payment details. The app checks your subscription status with Apple on your device, and when you use an AI feature it sends Apple's signed proof of your subscription so our server can confirm it (see Fair use and abuse prevention).

Website waitlist

If you join the waitlist on our website, we store your email address, the date you joined, and how you found us (for example, from TikTok). We use your email only to tell you when VoltAI launches and for occasional launch news. We don't share or sell it. When someone joins, we get an alert with the total count and source only, never the email. To be removed, email us and we'll delete it. The app doesn't use or connect to your waitlist email.

Consumer health data

Some information VoltAI handles, such as body and skin-health estimates, goals, activity level, and diet, may be considered "consumer health data" under laws like Washington's My Health My Data Act. Specifically:

  • What: the photos and details listed in AI analysis, and the results produced from them.
  • Why: only to provide the scans, plans, and recipes you request.
  • Shared with: Supabase and Anthropic as our processors, as described above. Never sold.
  • Your consent: collected in the app before any data is sent, and you can withdraw it anytime in Profile.
  • Your rights: you can confirm what we hold, ask us to delete it, and withdraw consent by contacting us. Since results and profiles are stored only on your phone, you can also delete them directly in the app.

Your choices and rights

  • Withdraw AI permission: Profile → Privacy → Withdraw. VoltAI will ask again before sending anything.
  • Delete your data: delete individual scans in the app, or delete the app to remove everything stored on your phone.
  • Waitlist: ask us to remove your email anytime.
  • Subscriptions: manage or cancel anytime in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU, UK, or California and other U.S. states), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Because VoltAI doesn't store your app data on its servers, most of it is under your direct control on your device. To exercise any right or ask a question, email us; we'll respond within the time required by law and won't discriminate against you for using your rights. Where the GDPR applies, we rely on your consent to process photos and health-related information, and on our legitimate interests to keep the service secure. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

All data sent from the app travels over encrypted HTTPS connections. Our server's AI credentials are kept in secure server-side storage and never ship in the app. No system is perfectly secure, but by not storing your photos or results on servers, we keep the amount of data that could ever be exposed to a minimum.

Age

VoltAI is not intended for people under 18 years of age.

Wellness, not medical advice

VoltAI provides general fitness and wellness estimates and suggestions. It isn't a medical service, its scores can be wrong, and it doesn't replace advice from a doctor or other qualified professional. Talk to a doctor before starting a new diet or exercise program, or if you have any health concerns.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects what's sent for AI analysis or who receives it, VoltAI will ask for your permission again in the app before sending anything.
