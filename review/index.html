VoltAI sample photos for App Review

Use these photos to test VoltAI on a device without taking new pictures.

How to use them
  1. Open this page in Safari on the test iPhone.
  2. Touch and hold each photo, then tap Save to Photos (or tap Download and save it).
  3. In VoltAI, tap Scan → New scan → Start → Agree and continue, then tap Library on each step and choose the matching photo.
  4. For Fridge AI (VoltAI Pro), tap Library and choose the fridge photo, or just type a few foods such as "chicken, rice, broccoli" and tap Find recipes.

Results take about 20–40 seconds. The AI asks for a retake only if a photo is too dark, blurry, or doesn't show a clothed person.
